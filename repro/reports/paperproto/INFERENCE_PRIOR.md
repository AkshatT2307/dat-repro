# Inference gallery: models trained before the Kaggle phase (kratos, protocol P)

Generated by `kgl/inference_gallery_prior.py` from the evaluation outputs saved when these models were evaluated (19–20 Nov 2026, RTX 5070 Ti). Checkpoints were not part of the copied archive; the final weights are on Hugging Face (`glitch23/fast-idr-paper-protocol`). Image PSNR is on uint8 as in the evaluation; seismic SNR in dB. Seismic panels: middle held-out test section, test-noise seed 0; removed/error panels use a scale tied to the added noise (printed in the titles). Rendering uses nearest-neighbour (no anti-aliasing).

## Contents

1. Image models (A1–A9)
2. Joint-corpus seismic models (C1–C9)
3. Field models (F1–F4)
4. Networks vs classical baselines
5. F3 and Stratton on real field noise
